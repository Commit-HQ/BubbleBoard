# Changelog

What's new in each version of BubbleBoard, newest first. The version an installation runs is in the footer of every page.

## 1.5.0 (2026-10-18)

- Tapping a notification opens the app at what it's about: the notice, event, board photo, message, or meeting comes into view, and its border glows for a moment.
- Teachers can book a meeting time for a family. The family sees that their teacher booked it, and can cancel it as usual.
- Files attached to a notice or an info page open from the form, before and after posting, and pictures show a small preview there.

## 1.4.0 (2026-10-12)

- Parents get a reminder the afternoon before their individual meeting, if they have notifications turned on.
- A parent's meeting card shows their booked time clearly, with a button to change or cancel it.

## 1.3.1 (2026-10-12)

- Changing a day's meeting times works while one of them starts: a time that started stays as it is, instead of the change being refused, and the open form keeps what the teacher changed.

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-12)

- Teachers add meeting times one by one, each with its own start and end, so a break or a longer meeting is simply a time set that way. The pencil on a day changes its free times later.
- Notifications reach Android phones while they sleep, instead of only once the phone is picked up.

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-12)

- The board shows notices and events together, newest first, whatever kind each is.
- Teachers can pin a notice to the top of the board. A pinned notice hangs from a pushpin until it's unpinned.
- Parents mark events as seen, as they do notices, and teachers see which families did.

## 1.1.1 (2026-09-27)

- Security fixes: group leads can only change the families and cards of their own groups, as intended.
- Limits that keep notifications from being flooded: a family can book or cancel meetings a few times a minute, and keeps up to 20 devices connected at once.

## 1.1.0 (2026-09-27)

- Back returns to the same place on the board, or on whichever page you came from, instead of the top.
- A child's face visibility has a History: when it was allowed or covered, and by whom, for parents in Settings and for staff on the child's page.
- While naming faces in event photos, a teacher sees which children's faces the classroom's families may see, the classroom's list of children shows it for every child, and the review before publishing lists every child's face visibility.
- Parents and teachers can hide notices and events they're done with. Hidden ones wait folded away at the bottom of the board, where Show on the board brings each back.
- Parents can report an event's photos, picking the ones they mean. The teachers get a notification and the report in Messages, and can take the photos down.
- Any teacher of a group can change or remove its notices and events, not only whoever posted them. Teachers see who edited a post.
- A clearer Messages inbox. Photo reports have their own section with a red flag. Teachers see each child's initials in the child's own colour, with a dot when a parent is waiting for an answer. Parents see whether the teachers have answered, and every message preview says who wrote it.
- When a teacher writes outside the classroom's hours, the parent can answer that message at any time. The teacher is told so while writing.

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-24)

The first release, in use at the Bubbles' kindergarten.

- QR cards instead of passwords: one printed card for a family's devices, one for each teacher.
- A notice board with rich notices, polls, and files, plus a photo of the real corkboard.
- Event photos, with faces covered by stickers on the teacher's phone. Each family decides who may see their child.
- Private questions between a family and their teachers.
- Individual meetings: teachers publish times, parents pick one.
- Notifications that say something is new, never what or about whom.
- Croatian and English, installable on phones and tablets.
